How a price is built

index_c = GNI_c ÷ GNI_anchor rule_c = index_c ^ ε (continuous) = tier multiplier (income tiers) mult_c = clamp(rule_c, floor, cap) price_c = anchor € × mult_c × (1 + channel adj.) doses_c = target pop × uptake × share combo € = flu comp. + COVID comp. + captured % × saved admin cost

The anchor is the net (ex-factory, after rebates) price you would accept in a market of average EU income. The rule then scales it with relative income. GNI per capita is the World Bank measure used for Gavi, UNICEF and WHO tiered vaccine pricing; the PPP variant compresses the EU spread from about 6× to about 2.7×, so it is the more conservative choice when external reference pricing is a concern.

The floor, cap and corridor alert reflect what the literature and practice say about Europe: most member states reference each other’s prices, so a large spread invites parallel trade and reference-price contagion. Moderate elasticity (0.3–0.6) with a floor of about 0.6–0.7× is a common industry compromise.

The combo anchor follows a “sum of the parts plus a share of the efficiency gain” logic. It mirrors the operational value hypotheses in the mCOMBRIAX access proposal: one administration instead of two, less cold-chain space and fewer consumables.

Presets

  • Single EU price: ε = 0. The joint-procurement logic of the EU COVID-19 APAs, one price for all 27.
  • ERP-safe corridor: PPP, ε = 0.35, floor 0.75, cap 1.15. Keeps max ÷ min near 1.5×.
  • Moderate tiering: Atlas GNI, ε = 0.5, floor 0.6, cap 1.3. Default.
  • Ability to pay: Atlas GNI, ε = 1.0, floor 0.4, cap 1.6. Closest to Ramsey or value-based differential pricing; highest spill-over risk.

Channel archetypes

From the p-valorem proposal in the Tender folder: A tender only (ES, SE, PL), B tender + retail pharmacy (FR, IE, PT), C tender + pharmacy + municipal or regional purchasers (DE, AT, IT). The other 18 countries are assigned as a starting assumption; change them in the table. Tender-only markets default to −10% because centralised MEAT or price-only awards compress net price; multi-layer markets default to +5%.

Data

  • GNI per capita, Atlas method (current US$), 2024, and GNI per capita PPP (current int’l $), World Bank World Development Indicators, via the nominal and PPP compilations.
  • Population and age structure: Eurostat, 1 January 2024, rounded. 50–64 share approximated.
  • Flu vaccination rate, 65+: latest OECD / ECDC values, rounded. Use as a starting point and overwrite with national data.
